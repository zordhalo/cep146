## Exercise 2: Recipe Card

# Late-Night Garlic Pasta

## Ingredients

- 200 g spaghetti
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- Salt, to taste
- Fresh parsley, chopped
- Grated parmesan, to finish

## Steps

1. Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water until *al dente*.
2. While it cooks, warm the olive oil over low heat and gently cook the garlic.
3. Add the chili flakes and cook for another 30 seconds.
4. Reserve half a cup of pasta water, then drain the pasta.
5. Toss the pasta into the garlic oil, adding pasta water a splash at a time.
6. Finish with parsley and parmesan, and serve immediately.

**Tip:** Keep the heat **low** when cooking the garlic. Burnt garlic turns
bitter and there is no saving the sauce once it does.

**Tip:** Always **save some pasta water**. The starch in it is what turns the
oil into a sauce that clings to the noodles.

# Academic Integrity Policy

## Purpose

To maintain high academic integrity standards and to provide a foundation for
research, teaching, learning, and working practices across the institution.

## Key Definition

> **Academic integrity** is a commitment, even in the face of adversity, to six
> fundamental values: **honesty**, **trust**, **fairness**, **respect**,
> **responsibility**, and **courage**.

## Key Definitions

- **Cheating:** Obtaining credit through dishonest means, including
  self-plagiarism, impersonation, falsification, unauthorized collaboration, and
  undeclared AI use.
- **Contract Cheating:** When a student submits work created by a third party as
  their own for academic credit, potentially involving payment.
- **Falsification / Fabrication:** Misrepresenting academic work, forging
  documentation, or creating false information to gain academic advantage.
- **Impersonation:** Taking assessments on behalf of another individual with
  their consent.
- **Plagiarism:** Presenting another's work without proper attribution,
  including modified versions that swap in synonyms.
- **Self-Plagiarism:** Unauthorized reuse of one's own previous assignment work.
- **Unauthorized Aid:** Using non-approved supports, including peer
  collaboration, AI technology, or internet resources.
- **Undeclared AI Use:** Using artificial intelligence to generate part or all
  of an assessment without a disclosure statement.

## Policy

### 1. General

- Seneca upholds a learning community that values **honesty**, **fairness**,
  **trust**, **respect**, **responsibility**, and **courage**.
- Seneca is committed to high-quality education within a positive, equitable,
  and inclusive environment.

### 2. Academic Integrity Program

- Academic integrity ensures that credentials accurately demonstrate a student's
  knowledge and learning.
- An integrated program requires aligned policy, faculty training, and student
  programming.

### 3. Responsibilities

- Seneca provides information and resources to ensure understanding of academic
  integrity.
- Students must inform faculty of circumstances affecting their performance
  promptly.
- Students can report suspected violations following the procedures in Appendix A.

### 4. Sanctions

- Sanctions are applied according to the severity of the violation (see Appendix B).
- Prior to imposing sanctions, student records are consulted for **aggravating**
  or **mitigating** factors.
- Violations combined with Student Code of Conduct breaches are investigated
  through that separate process.

## Appendix A: Academic Integrity Procedure

*Information Gathering and Assessment Phase*

| Action | Duration | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Report violation to school/program designate | As soon as possible; within a reasonable timeframe | Faculty, invigilator, student, support staff |
| Inform student and discuss informally | Within a reasonable timeframe | Faculty, invigilator, support staff |
| Collect and retain assessment/evidence | Within 5 days | Faculty, invigilator, support staff |
| If no offense: inform student | Within 2 business days | Faculty |
| If offense found: inform student, commence formal process | Within 5 business days | Faculty |
